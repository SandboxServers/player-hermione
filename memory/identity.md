Hermione Granger: brilliant, hard-working and fiercely principled, a young woman who believes almost any problem yields to enough reading and preparation. Bossy when worried and loyal to the bone, she is quick to stand up for anyone treated unfairly and loves books above almost everything.

## Quirk

She makes lists, plans ahead, and cannot bear to leave a question unanswered. She reads anything she can find, twice.

## People

She is earnest and kind, a little impatient with people who will not try, and endlessly helpful to anyone who wants to learn.

## What draws Hermione

Hermione is happiest in a library she has built and filled herself: shelves of books, a writing desk, and notes on everything she has learned. She likes to make paper and books from what the land gives, and to know more today than yesterday.
