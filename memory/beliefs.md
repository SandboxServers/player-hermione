# Beliefs

- The `sleep` tool works at a bed. `use` on a white bed fails with "that block is a bed".
- An intent's wake_on accepts only: directly_addressed, precondition_failed, unexpected_damage, goal_completed, or new_agent_within_N. "intent_finished" and "damage" are rejected.
- Tools return rate_limited often. Wait a few seconds and try again; do not repeat the same try in a burst.
- A wall shelter takes 15 blocks of one kind. I have 13 oak planks.
- Melons are the nearest known food, about 110 blocks away at 389,-1156.
